# Paid Technical Writing Programs

A curated, link-checked list of companies and publications that pay developers to write technical articles.

Most lists like this rot fast: programs close, companies get acquired, and pages redirect to the homepage. Every link here was checked and resolved to a live program page on **2026-09-30**. Pay figures come from the program page or from community reports. Always confirm the current rate and whether the program is open before you pitch.

## How to get accepted

1. **Publish 2–3 samples first** on your own blog, Hashnode, dev.to, or Medium. Almost every program asks for links.
2. **Match the audience.** Read 3–5 recent posts on the target blog before pitching.
3. **Pitch before you write.** Send a title, a 3-line summary, an outline, the target reader, and links to your samples.
4. **Pick topics you have actually built.** Working code and real gotchas beat generic explainers.
5. **Pitch several programs at once, but send each idea to only one.**
6. **Follow up once after 2 weeks.** Replies usually take 1–4 weeks.
7. **Apply to agencies too.** They are the most reliable source of repeat work.
